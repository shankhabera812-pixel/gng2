Our Story

Made by people
who actually care.

We built Grill & Green around a simple idea: food that's actually good for you should actually taste incredible. Every bowl, sandwich, and smoothie is made fresh — from ingredients you can pronounce — by people who genuinely care about what lands on your plate.

We're a Worcester original. Not a chain, not a franchise — just a kitchen that believes in doing things right and a community worth feeding well.
